# `unit_ai`: what a unit decides by itself

Each unit has a unit AI. It takes its orders and the notifies that its actions post, picks its
targets, answers damage, flees, and runs its type's idle checks. Animals, villagers, soldiers,
siege, ships and towers each have their own type.

Only the units of one player run their full unit AI in a tick: the player whose turn it is. The
others only take their orders and notifies. The unit AI's timers and its draws from the AI stream
show in checkpoint word 9.

- `mod.rs` holds the `UnitAi` state, its notifies and its order queue.
- `unit.rs` is the unit AI's part of the object's update.
- `update.rs`, `idle_part.rs`, `retarget.rs` and `idle.rs` hold the full update, its idle part,
  the retarget and the idle checks.
- `notifies.rs`, `handle.rs` and `type_notifies.rs` process the notifies: `handle.rs` hands each to
  its unit-AI type's handler (`type_notifies.rs`), most of which pass it on to the base handler.
- `process_order.rs`, `order_kinds.rs` and `attack_orders.rs` process the orders by kind.
- `attacked.rs`, `damaged.rs` and `flight.rs` hold the answers to attacks and damage.
- `attack_pick.rs`, `attack_rules.rs` and `cared.rs` say what each type can attack and what it
  searches for.
- `best_target/` picks the best unit to attack. `task_finder/` picks a task for a target.
  `world_view/` is the unit AI's view of the world.
- `side.rs` defines `UnitSide`: what the unit AI asks of the world. The tests answer it with
  small stand-ins, the engine with `world_view/`.
- `ai_kinds.rs` names the order and notify kinds. A script generates it.

## In the game

- `UnitAIModule` and its types (`UnitAIModule.cpp`, `aiuaimod.cpp`, `taiuaimd.cpp`).
